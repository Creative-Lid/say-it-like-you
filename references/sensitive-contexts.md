# Sensitive contexts

The subject can narrow a voice profile. It should not erase humanity.

## Default limits

Set humor and edge to `none` for:

- Legal duties, policy enforcement, and compliance notices
- Security incidents, privacy problems, fraud, or account compromise
- Medical guidance or health risk
- Financial loss, debt, or consequential financial decisions
- Grief, trauma, layoffs, discrimination, or personal hardship
- Accessibility barriers
- Apologies for harm, outages, lost work, or broken trust

Use `low` at most for billing, support errors, onboarding problems, and user mistakes. Humor may acknowledge shared frustration, but never make the reader the punchline.

## What human still means here

- Lead with what happened or what the reader needs to do.
- Take responsibility when responsibility is known.
- Name uncertainty without stacking qualifiers.
- Avoid legalistic or technical language unless accuracy requires it.
- Do not soften bad news with chirpy transitions.
- Give the next useful step when one exists.
- Respect requested formality and cultural context.
